# Goofy Grove — Brainrot Harvest

An original 3D brainrot collection game for make.fun's roblox-brainrot-01
bounty ("Make the best AI Roblox brainrot game"). Catch absurd wild brainrots
in the Grove, carry them home on your head, bank them on your base pads and
watch them print coins every second — then climb seven rarity tiers, roll
Shiny/Golden/Void mutations, hatch eggs, buy upgrades, lock your base and
rebirth for a permanent income multiplier. Watch out: every few minutes the
Gobbler wakes up and hunts the richest carrier, rivals can tag your catch off
your head, and an unlocked base can be robbed.

Everything — cast, names, world, art direction — is original: Sir Bopple the
apple knight, Noodle Newt, Baron von Banana, DJ Cactus Prime, the Eternal
Bagel, GLOOB Devourer of Snacks, and ten more. Inspired by the
collect-and-defend genre, copying none of its characters or assets.

## The game

- **World** (generated by `tools/gen_project.py` into `default.project.json`,
  ~500 instances): a fenced grove meadow with trees, rocks, flowers and lamps,
  a stone plaza, six coloured base plots around a ring road, a shop stall,
  three egg nests, cast statues by the spawn, and a stone Gobbler statue.
- **Catch & carry:** wild brainrots (max 10) wander the grove with name plates
  showing rarity and $/s; walk into one to hoist it onto your head.
- **Bank & income:** step on your base's bank zone to place the catch on a
  pad; every placed brainrot pays its rate × mutation × rebirth multiplier
  every second. Bases start with 8 pads, upgradable to 14.
- **Economy:** 7 rarities (Common → GroveGod), 3 mutations (Shiny ×2, Golden
  ×5, Void ×10), 3 eggs (Grove/Royal/Cosmic) with rarity-weighted hatches, 4
  stackable upgrades, and rebirths costing 25,000 × (n+1)^1.7 for +75% income.
- **Threat loop:** tag other carriers to knock their catch loose; steal from
  unlocked bases; the Gobbler Hunt event targets the richest carrier for 20 s;
  base locks run 60 s with a 120 s cooldown. A ScreenGui HUD (built in code,
  clear of Roblox's own menus) shows coins, income, rebirths, the carried
  catch, event banners and the shop.

## Layout

- `src/shared/` — `BrainrotDefs` (cast + economy data), `Format`.
- `src/server/` — `Main.server` bootstrap, `PlayerState` (state + DataStore),
  `ModelForge` (procedural character models), `BaseManager`, `WildSpawner`,
  `CatchService` (catch/bank/tag/steal/Gobbler), `ShopService`,
  `ScreenshotStage` (the staging functions the screenshots use).
- `src/client/` — `Main.client` + `Hud` (the whole ScreenGui, in code).
- `main.ts` hands the game in: copies `game.rbxl`, `PLAY.json`, `src/` and
  `screenshots/` to `/output` (skipping dot-names) and writes `dossier.json`
  with `vendor/makefun-roblox.ts`, the kit's copy, unchanged.
- `PLAY.json` names the published experience (zeros until published).
- `SCREENSHOTS.md` — the owner's publish + capture checklist.

## Build & validate

```sh
python3 tools/gen_project.py                       # regenerate the world tree
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl # build the place (Rojo 7)
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Validated on this machine: Rojo builds the place (520 instances), all 12 Luau
scripts compile with the Luau toolchain, and the dossier reports **100% of
the place's script bytes matched to `src/`** (gate: ≥ 80%).

## How this entry is submitted

This bounty grades from a **witnessed recording**: the game must be built
inside `makefun record` sessions, and the final files' content must be seen in
the recorded working tree. This repository is the complete reference build —
the submitted entry's build session re-authors this source under the recorder,
then, once the experience is published (see SCREENSHOTS.md) and `PLAY.json`
names it, the kit's `submit` runs. The publish gate is the account owner's:
only the owner can set the experience Public, upload its icon/thumbnail, and
pass Roblox's age check.
